FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A Starter Site usually takes 2–3 weeks. Business sites take 4–6 weeks, and online stores or web apps 6–12 weeks depending on features. You'll get an exact timeline in your proposal.

Can my customers pay with GCash or Maya?

Yes. We integrate payment gateways like PayMongo, Xendit or Maya Business so customers can pay with GCash, Maya, credit/debit cards and online banking. We can also enable cash on delivery.

What are your payment terms?

50% downpayment to begin, and the remaining 50% when your site goes live. For bigger projects we split payments into milestones. We accept GCash, Maya, bank transfer, and PayPal or Wise for overseas clients.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. We'll help you register a .com, .ph or .com.ph domain and set up reliable hosting, all in your name, so you always own your website.

Do you work with clients outside my city, or abroad?

Absolutely. We work fully online with clients anywhere in the Philippines, as well as OFWs and overseas businesses selling to Filipino customers.

Can we talk in Filipino?

Oo naman! Consultations can be in English, Filipino or Taglish, whatever's most comfortable for you.
